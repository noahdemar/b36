B-36 Flight Deck

You are in the aircraft commander's seat, lined up on a 4,000 m runway at 262,500 lb. Six R-4360s turning, four J47s on request.

Takeoff checklist

  1. Release parking brakeP
  2. Piston throttles full (hold)PgUp
  3. Jets full (hold)Shift + PgUp
  4. Flaps one notchF
  5. Rotate at 140 mph, hold about 8 degrees nose upS / Down
  6. Gear up once climbingG

Steer with A / D (roll), Q / E (rudder), W / S (pitch), Home / End (trim). Right-drag to look around, left-click or drag cockpit levers and switches. C switches to third person (the mouse aims and the aircraft follows, War Thunder style; W A S D take over), R resets, Shift+T changes the time of day, H shows every key. Needs a keyboard and a GPU; first load is about 27 MB.

Moving around the aircraft

Tab opens the crew station list: pick one and you walk there yourself, down the ladder and through the tunnel if needed. Or press X to stand up and walk with W A S D (Shift faster, Ctrl crouch); press E or click a seat, the ladder or the hatch to use it (clicking from across the cabin walks you there). The ladder behind the engineer's station leads down to the navigator and radar observer. The crawl tunnel over the bomb bays starts at the round hatch on the left of the flight deck's aft bulkhead, behind the forward gunners (walk up to it and press E or click it); it ends at the aft compartment.
