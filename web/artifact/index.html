B-36 Flight Deck

You are in the aircraft commander's seat, lined up on a 4,000 m runway at 262,500 lb. Six R-4360s turning, four J47s on request.

Takeoff checklist

  1. Release parking brakeP
  2. Piston throttles full (hold)PgUp
  3. Jets full (hold)Shift + PgUp
  4. Flaps one notchF
  5. Rotate at 140 mph, hold about 8 degrees nose upS / Down
  6. Gear up once climbingG

Steer with A / D (roll), Q / E (rudder), W / S (pitch), Home / End (trim). Right-drag to look around, left-click or drag cockpit levers and switches. C toggles the outside view, R resets, H shows every key. Needs a keyboard and a GPU; first load is about 7 MB.

Moving around the aircraft

X stands you up. Walk with W A S D (Shift faster, Ctrl crouch), and press E or click a seat to sit there. The ladder behind the engineer's station leads down to the navigator and radar observer on the lower deck. Only the two pilot seats fly the aircraft so far.
